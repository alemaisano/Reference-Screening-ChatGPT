You are assisting a literature review on modeling approaches linking road networks / transport infrastructure with biodiversity connectivity.

TASK
For each paper, decide whether it should be included in the next review step based only on title, abstract, and keywords.

TOPIC OF INTEREST
We are interested in papers that are meaningfully relevant to BOTH:
1. road networks / roads / transport infrastructure / transport systems / road ecology / linear transport infrastructure
AND
2. habitat connectivity / ecological connectivity / landscape connectivity / biodiversity connectivity / habitat fragmentation / wildlife corridors / movement barriers / permeability / wildlife movement

INCLUDE A PAPER IF
- both domains are clearly relevant; and
- the paper analyzes, assesses, models, simulates, reviews, or discusses the relationship between transport/road infrastructure and biodiversity/ecological connectivity or fragmentation.

REJECT A PAPER IF
- it is only about transport, infrastructure, traffic, or engineering, with no meaningful biodiversity/ecological connectivity relevance;
- it is only about ecology, conservation, genetics, or species distribution, with no meaningful relation to roads or transport infrastructure;
- it mentions roads and biodiversity separately, but not in a way that is clearly relevant to connectivity, fragmentation, barriers, crossings, wildlife movement, or related interactions;
- the relevance is too weak, incidental, or generic.

IMPORTANT DECISION RULE
Use only:
- accepted
- rejected

Do NOT use any other decision label.

CONFIDENCE
Assign:
- high = relevance or irrelevance is clear from title/abstract/keywords
- medium = decision is reasonable but some ambiguity remains
- low = decision is weak or abstract is vague

MANUAL CHECK
Set needs_manual_check = "yes" if:
- confidence is low
- or the abstract is ambiguous
- or the paper is borderline relevant
- or roads/transport are only part of the broader landscape context
- or connectivity is only indirectly addressed

Set needs_manual_check = "no" if:
- the decision is clear and well supported by the title/abstract/keywords

STRICT RULES
- Be conservative and literal.
- Do not invent details.
- Do not assume a modeling component if it is not stated.
- Do not include papers just because they mention roads and ecology separately.
- Focus on meaningful relevance to the review scope.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Return ONLY valid JSON with exactly this structure:

{
  "results": [
    {
      "code": "exact input ID",
      "decision": "accepted or rejected",
      "reason": "short explanation",
      "confidence": "high or medium or low",
      "needs_manual_check": "yes or no"
    }
  ]
}
